A **skill** is a short set of instructions you give an AI: how you like things written, your refund policy, your code-review checklist, the steps for a launch. Skillet stores one copy of each skill you own, writes it into every AI tool you use, and lets you run skills from people you trust.

## Find skills worth running

Skillet ranks skills by the people you follow, not by install count. Follow someone whose taste you trust and their published skills are one click from your kit, or [publish your own](/docs/publish).

Once skills are in your kit, `/skillet <task>` picks the right one for the job. You can also run someone's skills before adding anything: `/skillet @handle <task>`, or one pasted URL with nothing installed at all. See [Summon a kit](/docs/summon). Usage recording is opt-in and never includes your prompt or task. [What `/skillet` records](/docs/privacy) lists exactly what is stored.

## Keep skills current everywhere

Skillet keeps one copy of each skill and writes it into every runtime you connect: Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, and more, each in the format that runtime expects. Edit a skill once and every runtime gets the update. Updates from other people arrive as diffs, and nothing lands until you approve it. [Runtimes](/docs/runtimes) lists every supported tool.

## Keep your team on the same set

A team shares one private kit. Incoming changes arrive as diffs for approval, and every member and every tool stays on the same version. [Teams and shared kits](/docs/teams) covers setup and roles.

## Get set up

The website handles sign-in, publishing, kits, teams, follows, and update approval. Writing skills onto your machine requires a sync client:

- [Install](/docs/install) the Mac or Windows app, or the CLI if you prefer the terminal. Either one keeps your tools current and surfaces updates to approve.

[Skills and kits](/docs/skills-and-kits) covers the whole model in two nouns and two verbs. [Runtimes](/docs/runtimes) lists every supported tool.

> **Good to know**
> Skillet is free to start. Your skills live on your machine in `~/.skillet`, and they are yours. Updates from other people are [scanned, not certified](/docs/safety), and a human approves every change before it lands.
