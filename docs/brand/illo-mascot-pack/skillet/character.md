# Skillet — custom character (portrait bust)

The SkilletMD chef mascot, exactly as the logo: a **head-and-shoulders portrait**
of a round-headed cook in a tall toque, winking with a terminal-prompt `>` eye.
The name reads off the design (a chef = skillet).

Style: **clean-flat (custom — brand vinyl line, not riso)**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: mascot
Sibling pack: `skillet-full` (full-body version for editorial scenes)

## Locked design

- **Framing**: a centered, front-facing **portrait bust** — head, toque, and the
  tops of the shoulders only; the body is cropped at the bottom edge. NOT a
  full-body figure.
- **Head**: a rounded **oval, slightly WIDER than tall** — never a perfect circle
  and never a fat ball.
- **Toque**: a tall three-lobe chef's hat (a soft cloud of three rounded puffs)
  sitting on top of the head; its lower band narrows into two short vertical sides
  that tuck just behind the head. The toque is large — roughly as tall as the head.
- **Face**: a bold, softly-rounded chevron `>` for the winking left eye; a small
  open ring (a hollow circle outline, bold even stroke, small white centre) for
  the right eye; a small shallow upturned smile centered below and between them.
  No eyebrows, nothing else.
- **Shoulders**: two symmetric curved strokes sweeping **down and outward** from
  behind the lower head — the shoulders/upper body running off the bottom of the
  frame. These are shoulders, not arms or legs.
- **Accent carrier**: the **toque** — the only part that ever takes accent color.

## Value rules

- Pure brand reading (default): white/paper fill, ONE bold even-weight near-black
  outline for head, toque, face, and shoulders. Optional: fill the toque with the
  accent color; everything else stays ink-on-paper.
- **Dark palettes**: head/shoulders stay light with the structure-ink outline (an
  outline character, never an ink blob); features in structure ink; toque accent.

## Visual style (clean-flat — substitute for riso blocks in every prompt)

- **LINE LANGUAGE**: ONE bold, even-weight, softly-rounded outline — a clean
  vinyl-sticker / icon line, every stroke the same weight as the brand logo.
  Nothing thin, scratchy, or tapered.
- **STYLE**: clean flat vector — solid flat fills, crisp edges, NO halftone, NO
  grain, NO ink-layer offset, NO gradients, NO soft shadows.

## Personality

An earnest, unflappable cook who keeps the plates flying with a wink — calm
competence, never zany. Expression lives in the wink; the idea is carried by
context, not by exaggerated faces.
